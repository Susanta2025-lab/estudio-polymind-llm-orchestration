## Rubric and acceptance

Apply all seven dimensions independently to the structural and root outputs, plus leaf support as an input-quality check. Structural validity and semantic judgments have separate fields.

| Dimension | Acceptance condition and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Grounded factual support | At least 95% expert-supported factual claims, explicit numerator/denominator and per-claim source/child support mapping; zero critical unsupported conclusions. Split compound assertions into atomic propositions for review while retaining original claim IDs/text. |
| Important-information coverage | At least 90% of the four frozen major findings, all 19 unit/valve pairs and the material pre-operation condition. Report partial findings, omissions, wrong associations and severity separately. |
| Standalone usefulness | A domain reader can identify the inspection subject, units, required actions and scope without decoding wrappers or request-local aliases. Valid lineage alone does not satisfy this dimension. |
| Cross-section synthesis | Structural output explains the fallback section's actual inspection content. Root integrates the shared requirement across the four units while retaining unit-specific inventory. No invented causal, temporal, safety or universal relationship; record unsupported or not-applicable relationships explicitly. |
| Qualification preservation | Preserve every material timing condition, exception, uncertainty and conflict actually supported by source or accepted children. Do not turn an instruction into a successful inspection result or absence of evidence into certainty. |
| Provenance validity | All production G1/G2, source membership, immediate-child selection, application-owned identities, receipt/request/table association, fenced manifests and publication citations pass; independently assess relevance/entailment for each selected support. |
| Conciseness and readability | Useful domain content dominates; group repeated requirements without losing the inventories or qualifications. Avoid wrapper counts, opaque aliases, redundant restatement and unnecessary machinery. Literal source-domain identifiers remain valid. |

Require every structural gate, preservation of all material conditions, zero critical unsupported conclusions and both numerical thresholds. Do not average away a failure. A structural reducer that discusses how many children it processed fails usefulness even if G2 passes. A root that only echoes wrapper statistics or merely repeats structural processing prose fails synthesis. Reusing a supported factual statement where this homogeneous source warrants it is not automatically a failure; reviewers must explain the missing integration or redundancy.
