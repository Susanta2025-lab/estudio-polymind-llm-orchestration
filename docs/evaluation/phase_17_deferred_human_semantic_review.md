coverage, standalone usefulness, cross-section synthesis, qualification preservation,
provenance validity, and conciseness/readability. Required targets remain at least
95% expert-supported factual assertions, at least 90% important-finding coverage,
zero critical unsupported conclusions, every material qualification preserved,
relevant/valid supporting evidence and useful, understandable structural/root
synthesis. The protocol additionally requires all 19 unit/valve pairs and the
material pre-operation condition. Keep every structural gate; do not average away
a failure. Two independent assessments and necessary adjudication are mandatory.

## Deferred review completion procedure

1. Supply each reviewer the unchanged source and actual accepted v11 outputs,
   with their evidence bindings. Preserve original versions; mask version labels
   where practical and obtain judgments without showing prior PASS conclusions
   or the assistant assessment as an answer key.
2. Obtain independent judgments from two suitably qualified human reviewers.
3. Apply the frozen seven-dimensional rubric and its unchanged targets.
4. Record claim-level support, omissions, material qualifications and relevance
   of each selected source/child, retaining original claim texts and identities.
5. Record reviewer identities or approved IDs, expertise, independence and dates
   in protected review evidence; publish only an approved sanitized reference.
6. Retain both original reviews and document adjudication of material disagreements.
   Unresolved consequential disagreements leave acceptance pending.
7. Issue a documented final semantic acceptance decision with numerators,
   denominators, exclusions, not-applicable judgments and rationale.
8. Update the register, completion decision, README/current trackers and formal
   Phase 17 closure status based on the accepted decision and applicable gates.
