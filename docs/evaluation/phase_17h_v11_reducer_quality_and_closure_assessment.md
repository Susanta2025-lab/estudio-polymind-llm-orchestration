## Reusable rubric: reducer-semantic-quality/1

Review both structural sections and the complete root synthesis. Freeze a source
manifest and annotate major findings, material qualifications and consequential
relationships **before** judging the output. Keep deterministic structural checks
and independent semantic decisions as separate fields. A valid E/C selection
proves membership and relationship identity, not entailment or citation relevance.

| Dimension | Observable semantic pass/fail rule | Required reviewer evidence | Deterministic contribution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grounded factual support | Every consequential conclusion is entailed by supplied support; no critical unsupported technical, legal or causal conclusion | Per-claim source spans, accepted immediate-child texts/links, support judgment and severity; distinguish inference from explicit source fact | Exact scoped coordinates, authorized reference membership, claim identity, no cross-request transplantation |
| Important-information coverage | Retain at least 90% of independently preannotated major findings, with no omitted safety-critical requirement; identify omissions explicitly | Finding inventory, importance weights/rationale, output claim mapping and retained/omitted labels | Fixture-specific labelled phrases and complete source/child coverage; structural coverage is not semantic recall |
| Standalone usefulness | A domain reader can identify the subject, main supported conclusions and practical significance without decoding local aliases or understanding reducers | Reader judgment and examples of missing context; explain any necessary domain terminology | Negative metadata/wrapper examples; meaningful source identifiers such as C1 remain allowed |
| Cross-section synthesis | Accurately combine related findings and preserve scope/distinctions; no invented causal, temporal or universal relationship; mark not-applicable when no cross-section relationship is supported | Annotated relationships and section boundaries, exact supporting findings, explanation of the synthesis and unsupported leaps | Exact immediate-child membership and grandchild rejection; no automated entailment guarantee |
| Qualification preservation | Retain every material uncertainty, exception, conflict, limitation and unresolved question; never convert absence of evidence into certainty | Preannotated caveats/conflicts, text mapping, preserved/lost/strengthened labels and conflict-resolution authority | Inherited qualification/contradiction propagation and fixture labels |
| Provenance validity | All selected sources/children are authorized, complete and stage-correct; raw record/receipt/checkpoint association verifies; no fabricated direct authority | Machine validation records plus source relevance review | G1/G2, request binding, deterministic identity, immutable hashes, fences, replay, coverage and receipt checks must all pass |
| Conciseness and readability | Useful findings dominate prose; avoid equivalent repetition and irrelevant counts/wrappers/aliases while preserving consequential detail | Reviewer assessment with examples of redundancy, missing meaning or misleading compression | Byte/token/child limits and fixture checks; length compliance alone is insufficient |

The proposed Phase 17A targets of at least 95% expert-supported claims, at least
90% major findings and zero critical unsupported conclusions remain **targets**,
not newly measured scores. Report numerators, denominators, exclusions and
not-applicable judgments. Require all structural gates, all material caveats and
zero critical unsupported conclusions; do not average a critical failure away.
An initial trained domain reviewer annotates claims and omissions; a separate
reviewer checks consequential findings, qualifications and disagreements. Retain
both judgments and an adjudication rationale rather than silently replacing them.
No such independent review is completed by this implementation task.

For larger benchmarks retain rubric version, source/configuration/generation
fingerprints, domain, physical pages, structural depth, call topology, raw outputs,
exact support annotations, independent reviewer identities, disagreements and
final decisions. Include difficult cross-section relationships, tables, negative
results, supersession and conflicts, alongside straightforward procedures. Treat
reliability and operational performance as separate measurements.
