## Future acceptance procedure

When the operator chooses actual external testing, retain protected evidence for each step:

1. Select the approved pilot document and participant scope. Freeze exact source/version/hash, cohort, supervision and limits; do not infer approval from implemented capacity.
2. Validate source-processing rights and eligible document classification through `SourceApproval`. Possession/public availability is insufficient. Exclude private/personal, OCR/essential-graphics/complex-layout and erasure-required sources outside the implemented contract.
3. Complete relevant privacy, consent and retention decisions: actual notice/purpose/contact, voluntary consent and withdrawal, provider terms/region/processing rights, class-specific custody/deadlines and acknowledged residuals. Complete private `SessionApproval`; templates are not approvals.
4. Obtain applicable independent quality evidence for the exact retained or newly authorized actual digest/answer and source spans. Apply the unchanged accepted rubric and adjudicate material disagreements. External-mode digest release needs two accepted exact-output findings; a new answer needs its own two reviews before `present_answer`. Reviewer assertions in code require operator/domain verification of qualification/independence.
5. Validate current verified operator/publication authority, existing budgets/history/UNKNOWN obligations, catalog, provider capacity and operational stop controls. Use the same bound ledgers/admission authority, updated shared consumers and current forecasts; do not reset quotas. A forecast reserves nothing.
6. Obtain separate participation/exposure authorization and paid-execution authorization where required, including exact scope/model/calls/tokens/window/caps and the applicable independent signing procedure. An approved zero-call retained-result protocol grants no new Q&A allowance. Default `fixture_execution_only` cannot authorize real inference; a future real-provider composition needs its separately reviewed per-call authorization integration.
7. Execute controlled approved sessions through the [operator reference](phase_17i_operator_workflow.md), within all applicable accepted [G01–G16 gates](phase_17i_external_user_readiness_gates.md#post-implementation--stage-3-reconciliation--13-november-2026). Stop on any hard safety violation or uncertain usage. Preserve failures/withdrawals in denominators.
8. Collect real usefulness, correctness, citation and error feedback, notice understanding and repeat willingness. Keep original protected observations, including dissent; synthetic feedback never enters the real denominator.
9. Reconcile usage and participant/session records against exact task/source/job/checkpoint/generation/citation evidence. Preserve known costs and UNKNOWN obligations; do not invent refunds or final provider invoice values.
10. Complete withdrawal/closure, revocation, display/export handling and residual-retention accounting. Apply the approved metadata policy and record `PURGE_PENDING` where physical erasure cannot be proven, including provider/backups/SQLite residuals.
11. Apply the accepted Phase 17I criteria from the [existing plan](phase_17i_implementation_and_verification_plan.md#candidate-study-and-acceptance-thresholds), identifying which proposals the operator actually approved. Preserve the current quality thresholds and every hard gate; report numerators, denominators, exclusions, failures and limitations.
12. Issue the final external-user verification decision with accepted evidence references and operator ownership. Update this obligation, the completion decision, gate register and README consistently. Formal acceptance requires accepted actual evidence or an explicitly operator-approved revised acceptance policy; Stage 3 authorizes neither revision nor execution.
